The **Thesaurus** is your organization's own vocabulary to classify content. It is made of **terms** (also called concepts), ordered in **categories** and **subcategories**. Each term has its definition, its labels in each language, its synonyms, its broader and narrower terms, its related terms, a scope note and its internal category.

It is not a general dictionary: it only has the terms your organization adds. The **Keywords V2** tool works from it, so it needs your organization to have one.

## From the platform

Open **Thesaurus** in the left menu (the page is titled **Thesauri**).

- **Browse.** The categories are on the left; open one to see its subcategories and terms. **Search term...** looks for a term, **Search categories...** for a category, and **Alphabetical search** goes by letter. **Statistics** shows how big the vocabulary is.
- **Add.** **New term**, **New category** (and **Add subcategory**) create them. A term needs its English label and a parent category (*English term is required*, *You must select a parent category*).
- **Edit a term.** Open it to see its sheet, with:
  1. identity of the concept (its ID),
  2. definition,
  3. labels by language (the preferred label of each language),
  4. synonyms by language (variants of the same concept, not new concepts),
  5. hierarchy: **Broader (BT)** and **Narrower (NT)**,
  6. related terms (**RT**),
  7. scope note (what the concept includes and excludes),
  8. internal category,
  9. semantic usage (**Used for**),
  10. prompts for AI and visual use.

  Use **Add synonym** to add a synonym, and **Save term** to save. **Delete term** removes it.
- **Categories.** **Rename category** and **Delete category**. You can only delete categories that are empty and were created manually; one that contains terms or subcategories cannot be deleted.

## By API

There is no public API for the thesaurus. It is managed in the platform, and the tools that use it read it by themselves; you do not send it in the request.
