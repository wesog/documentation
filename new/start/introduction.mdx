Wesog is a platform to **find, enrich and move your photos, videos and documents**. You keep them in a **Library**, search them in plain language, let AI tools describe and tag them, and chain those steps into **workflows** that run on their own.

Everything you can do in the platform you can also do from your own software through the **API**. This documentation follows the same order as the platform menu, so that you always know where a page lives in the product and where a feature lives in the docs.

## How the documentation is organized

| Section of the docs | In the platform | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Get started** (this section) | - | Quickstart, authentication, errors, limits and plans, and the rules shared by the whole API. |
| **Search** | **Search**, **Active searches**, **Connect an agent** | Searching your Library, the conversational search, a search box on your own website, alerts for new matches and connecting ChatGPT, Claude or your own agent. |
| **Workflows** | **Workflows**, **Reviews** | Automations made of steps: templates, the Studio, runs, and the review of results before they are saved. |
| **Tools** | **Tools** | The AI tools you can run on a file, one by one, by screen or by API. |
| **Entities** | **Entities** | People, places, brands and other things Wesog recognizes, and your own private ones. |
| **Thesaurus** | **Thesaurus** | The controlled vocabulary used to tag your content. |
| **Library** | **Library** | Uploading content (web, API, FTP or S3), editing metadata, trash and restore. |
| **Settings** | The organization menu, **Credentials**, **Subscriptions** | Your organization and users, API keys, FTP and S3 access, connections, plan and billing. |
| **Changes** | - | What changed and when, only what you can notice as a customer. |

## Your organization

An **organization** is your account space in Wesog: its users, its Library, its workflows, its API keys, its plan and its limits. Everything you upload or create belongs to the organization, not to a single person. One person can belong to several organizations and switches between them from the menu at the top of the sidebar.

Your **plan** decides how many users, how much storage, how many requests, searches and credits you have. See [Limits and plans](/new/start/limits-and-plans).
