A **search alert** repeats a search for you and notifies you when new files match: *"new photos of the Madrid fashion week"*. It is the way to react to new content without polling the API.

Alerts belong to a **person**, not to an API key, so they are created and managed in the app or from an agent connected by [MCP](/new/guides/connect-agents), not with REST calls and an API key.

## Create an alert

- **In the app:** on the Search page ask for it in your words (*"tell me when there are photos of Rosalía"*) or run a search and press **Notify me of new ones** under the results.
- **From ChatGPT, Claude or your agent:** *"alert me when new photos of the fashion week arrive"*.

In both cases Wesog shows you how it understood the search and where it will look. **The alert does not exist until you confirm it**, so that an alert that understood the wrong thing never sends noise.

## How it behaves

- It only tells you about files that arrive **after** it was created, and never twice about the same file.
- You choose how often: **Immediate** (within seconds), **Hourly** (the default) or **Daily** (one summary a day).
- Notices arrive in the app's notification bell; tick **Also email me a summary** to get an email too.
- An alert never expires unless you set an end date (1 week, 1 month or a date). When it ends it pauses by itself.
- On the **Alerts** page (and from your agent) you can pause, resume, change the frequency and delete each one, and see what it found.

## Limits

Your plan sets how many active alerts you can have and how many can be immediate. The Free plan has none. Paused alerts do not count. See your numbers in **Current tenant settings > Subscription**.

## If you need to react from your program

Use one of these two patterns:

- Let the alert tell a person (bell or email), and have the person or your agent open the results.
- Make your program [search](/new/guides/search) on a schedule, asking for the latest files (`sort` by `created_desc` with an empty `query`, or a `created_at` date range in `filters`) and remember the last `resource_id` you processed.
